Code of Conduct

Inclusive Development Environment
Required

We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for all developers, regardless of:

  • Gender, gender identity, or gender expression
  • Technical skill level or experience
  • Race, ethnicity, or nationality
  • Age, disability, or physical appearance

Discrimination or harassment of any kind will not be tolerated.

Technical Conduct
Important

When providing technical help or reviewing code:

  • Explain concepts clearly and patiently
  • Provide constructive feedback
  • Don't assume prior knowledge
  • Use proper code formatting
  • Cite sources when sharing solutions
