1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better services to our users:

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, username, and password.
  • Profile Information: You may provide additional information like your profile picture, phone number, and bio.
  • Learning Data: We track your course progress, quiz scores, assignments, and certificates.
  • Payment Information: For paid courses, we collect payment details through secure payment processors.
  • Technical Information: We collect IP addresses, browser type, device information, and usage data.
2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain our learning platform
  • To personalize your learning experience
  • To process payments and prevent fraud
  • To communicate with you about platform updates, new courses, and promotions
  • To improve our services and develop new features
  • To comply with legal obligations
3. Data Protection

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information:

  • Data encryption in transit and at rest
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Access controls and authentication systems
  • Secure payment processing through certified providers

Despite our efforts, no security measures are 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

4. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: You can request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: You can update or correct inaccurate information
  • Deletion: You can request deletion of your personal data
  • Objection: You can object to certain processing of your data
  • Portability: You can request transfer of your data to another service

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@codelab.com.

5. Cookies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to:

You can control cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling cookies may affect your experience on our platform.
